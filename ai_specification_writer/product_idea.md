# Product Idea: AstemLab Urban Grid Analytics

## Vision
Empower municipal transit authorities and urban infrastructure operators to harvest wasted low-grade thermal energy from subway tunnels and industrial shafts, converting urban heat losses into self-powered IoT analytics and sustainable district heating.

## Users
- **Municipal Transit & Infrastructure Operators**: Need real-time airflow, groundwater, and structural thermal monitoring inside subway tunnels without paying for expensive grid wiring or frequent battery replacements.
- **District Heating & Utility Providers**: Want reliable, continuous access to localized low-grade thermal energy to boost heat pump efficiency and reduce municipal carbon emissions.
- **Urban Sustainability & Smart-City Planners**: Need actionable, verified data on urban heat islands and energy recovery metrics to meet climate targets and secure green infrastructure funding.

## Key Features & Differentiators
- **Self-Powered Thermoelectric IoT Nodes**: Harvests waste heat directly from tunnel walls and exhaust shafts to power wireless environmental sensors autonomously.
- **AI-Governed Heat Pump Optimization**: Dynamically adjusts thermal transfer rates to upgrade 25°C–35°C waste heat into usable 60°C–90°C district heating water with a COP of 3.8+.
- **Real-Time Telemetry & Predictive Maintenance Dashboard**: Tracks tunnel airflow, groundwater intrusion, and thermal output in real time, alerting engineers to anomalies before hardware failure occurs.
- **Zero-Disruption Modular Retrofit**: Designed to mount cleanly onto existing transit ventilation shafts without interrupting daily train operations.
