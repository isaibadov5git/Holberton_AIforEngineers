# Team Ideation

## Brainstormed Ideas
1. **AI Lab Safety & SDS Protocol Assistant**: An interactive tool that parses chemical Safety Data Sheets (SDS) into step-by-step PPE and spill-response checklists for engineering students.
2. **District Thermal Load & Waste-Heat Site Screener** ✅ (chosen): An AI-assisted site screening and feasibility calculator that evaluates municipal transit hubs and industrial exhaust shafts for waste-heat recovery potential, COP efficiency, and carbon savings.
3. **Automated Academic Citation & Literature Gap Mapper**: A research assistant that ingests engineering PDFs and generates structured comparison tables of methodologies and unresolved limitations.

## Problem Statement
Municipal energy planners and clean-tech engineers spend weeks manually compiling site parameters (exhaust temperature, airflow rate, operating hours, and target district heating demand) across spreadsheets to screen urban waste-heat recovery sites, leading to slow feasibility assessments and inconsistent economic projections.

## MVP Features
- **Site Parameter Input Form**: Allows engineers to enter transit or industrial site metrics (exhaust temperature in °C, volumetric airflow in m³/s, and target output temperature).
- **AI Thermodynamic & COP Feasibility Estimator**: Computes recoverable thermal power (kW), projected heat pump Coefficient of Performance (COP), and annual CO₂e offset with AI-generated site recommendations.
- **Multi-Site Comparison Dashboard**: Displays screened hubs side-by-side in a sortable table ranked by thermal yield and payback efficiency.
- **CSV Feasibility Report Export**: Generates a clean, downloadable `.csv` summary of screened sites and calculated metrics for stakeholder reviews.
